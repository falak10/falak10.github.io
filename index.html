E-Commerce Installment Payment AnalysisData: 2016–2018

I analyzed the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset from Kaggle to understand how customers pay, and whether paying in installments relates to order value and satisfaction.

Just over half of delivered orders were paid in installments. Boleto, a Brazilian bank-slip payment, came second to credit cards at about 2.8M, and vouchers were small. Monthly sales grew steadily from late 2016 into 2018.

Restaurant Review Sentiment AnalysisNov 2023 – Feb 2024

I built a binary sentiment classifier in R that labels restaurant reviews as positive or negative, then visualized which words customers use most.

The dataset is evenly split, with 500 positive and 500 negative reviews, so the model has no majority class to lean on.

Role: Analyst

View on GitHub

Clean the text.

Lowercased the reviews, removed punctuation and filtered stopwords with tm and dplyr.

Weight the words.

TF-IDF turns each review into features and gives more weight to words that carry meaning.

Train and test.

An 80/20 split keeps test reviews unseen, and caret's confusion matrix evaluates the predictions.

Show the language.

A ggplot2 bar plot of frequent terms and a wordcloud2 word cloud make the results easy to read.

Smart Waste Classification AppSep 2023 – May 2024

Raskalah is a mobile app that identifies waste from a photo, points people to nearby recycling centers, and rewards them with points.

We tested three pre-trained CNNs before and after fine-tuning. DenseNet121 held 97% at 30 and 50 epochs and fell to 84% by 60, so the number of epochs changed the result.
